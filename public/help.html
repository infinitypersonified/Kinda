Help, with a little more clarity.

Quick answers about signing in, finding tasks, choosing what to share, and managing your account.

Signing in

  • Use the same sign-in method you used when you created your account.
  • If you use email and password, choose Forgot password? on the login screen to request a reset link.
  • If Google sign-in returns an error, try the same Google account linked to your Kinda profile.

A task isn’t showing

  • In My Tasks, check the Today, Earlier, Upcoming, Completed, and No date filters.
  • Refresh Kinda when you have a connection. If the app shows its connection-lost screen, wait until it reconnects before trying again.
  • Kinda confirms a new task after the save request succeeds. If it still doesn’t appear after a refresh, try once more and note any error message.

What can other people see?

  • Your personal tasks stay private unless you choose to share them with a friend or circle.
  • Circle members can see tasks shared with that circle and messages sent in its chat.
  • A private calendar feed works like a secret link: anyone who has the link can see dated tasks. Revoke or regenerate it in Settings.
  • Google Calendar sync is limited to tasks created in Kinda; other Google Calendar events are not imported.

Your account and notifications

Open Settings → Your Data to export your account data or delete your Kinda account. Notification controls are also in Settings; browser permissions can be changed in your device or browser settings.
